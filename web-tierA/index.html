    FAQ

    What can I feed it?

    mp4 / mov / webm files up to 200 MB, or a YouTube / direct video link. Videos longer than 10 minutes are analyzed per segment — pick yours or let StatCam take the first 10.

    Which sports are supported?

    Soccer, basketball and running today. Tracking itself is sport-agnostic — if athletes and a ball are visible from a stable viewpoint, it works.

    Do I need wearables or special hardware?

    No. Any phone video filmed from the sideline works. No signups, no subscriptions, nothing to install.

    Are the numbers real?

    Every stat is computed from the tracked positions of real players in your video — the agent timeline shows each step. Results are always labeled with how they were produced.

    Where do my videos go?

    They are processed on our server for analysis only — never shared, never published. Clips and report cards are generated only for you.
